About the Project

What is AquaSense?

AquaSense is an intelligent, fully automated water quality monitoring system built on IoT architecture. It continuously measures key water parameters in real-time, making water safety analysis accessible, fast, and reliable.

⚠️

The Problem

Traditional water testing is manual, slow, expensive, and fails to detect sudden contamination events in time — putting communities at risk.

🔬

Our Solution

A network of smart sensors continuously monitors water quality parameters and transmits data wirelessly. Alerts are sent instantly when thresholds are breached.

🌍

The Impact

Safer drinking water for communities, reduced cost of water quality management, and early detection of pollution events through predictive analytics.

Core Features

How It Works

Six key capabilities that make AquaSense a next-generation water safety solution.

📡

Real-Time Sensing

Continuous data collection from multiple sensors deployed at various points in the water network, updating every few seconds.

🧪

Multi-Parameter Analysis

Monitors pH, turbidity, dissolved oxygen, temperature, TDS (Total Dissolved Solids), and conductivity simultaneously.

🔔

Instant Alerts

Automated alerts via SMS and email notify stakeholders the moment any parameter crosses safe thresholds.

🤖

AI-Driven Predictions

Machine learning models analyze historical sensor data to predict water quality trends and potential contamination events.

☁️

Cloud Storage & Logging

All sensor readings are securely stored in the cloud with timestamp accuracy, enabling long-term analysis and reporting.

📊

Interactive Dashboard

A beautiful, intuitive dashboard displays live and historical data through charts, gauges, and status indicators.
